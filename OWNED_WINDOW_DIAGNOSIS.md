
Capture support and permissions are independent diagnostics. The native process starts before screencapture help/admission and completes its unchanged 15-second acceptance even if help is unsupported, capture is refused, or capture times out. The outer 17-second cleanup bound is unchanged. Reports distinguish native presentation success from overall capture success. `--capture-failure-self-test` uses real bounded child processes with three simulated diagnostic failures and requires their completion marker, exit zero and absence of early termination; it makes no native execution claim.
